# Senior Art Director Review

Generation is not approval. The pre-render review cross-checks communication, brand, reference transformation, Creative Direction and deterministic Layout Intelligence. Mechanical blockers are rejected locally before any Sol call. Sol judges decision quality through a compact packet; local code validates evidence references and resolves the final verdict.

The critic may observe planned hierarchy, geometry, safe areas, topology and constraints. Image authenticity and material realism are only partial; raster artifacts, skin, hands, final shadows and sharpness are not observable before rendering. Claims about those properties are invalid.

Approval thresholds are centralized: 86 for approved and 82 for approved-with-notes, always with zero blockers and majors. `ReviewedDesignPackage` containing the final route and final layout is the production authority. `readyForRender` is true only for approved outcomes with local gates passing.

Sol uses the existing provider, structured output and durable `senior_critic` budget stage. Cache fingerprints exclude images and include creative/layout selections, brand/reference sessions, format, schema and critic policy.
